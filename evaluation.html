💡 9. What You Could Have Written

Improved Answer Approach

  1. Start with a concise context-setting introduction.
  2. Directly address the directive and core demand.
  3. Organise the body into clear dimensions.
  4. Use relevant constitutional provisions, reports, data, examples or case studies where applicable.
  5. Include balanced analysis rather than merely listing points.
  6. End with a concise, practical and optimistic way forward.
